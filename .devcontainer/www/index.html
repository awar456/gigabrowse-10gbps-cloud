Make it fast

  • Zoom a page: open the floating keyboard, then Ctrl++ / Ctrl+−.
  • Address bar: Ctrl+L, type, Enter.
  • Typing: tap the floating keyboard button (bottom of the screen) — the iOS keyboard can’t appear inside a streamed desktop; this is the trade-off that keeps the gigaspeed.
  • Downloads: just download normally. The watcher moves finished files to Drive by itself — nothing to do.
  • Full screen: the KasmVNC menu (left edge) → Fullscreen.

What this can’t do

  • Movies/shows bought on Apple TV or Amazon Prime can’t be downloaded as playable files — they’re DRM-protected streams. Their apps’ offline downloads are encrypted and locked to the app. Direct file downloads of any size work; DRM streams don’t. To truly own a movie: buy the disc and rip it to the Mini/Jellyfin.
  • The native iOS keyboard never appears inside the streamed browser. Floating keyboard instead — by design.
  • Codespaces stop after 30 min idle and 12 h max. The Mini restarts them on demand.
